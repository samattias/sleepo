# What's new in SleePO

Newest first. The website shows the latest three.

## 25 Nov 2026
- **Actions, the new first page.** What people are waiting on you for (emails that need a response, Teams and Slack messages
  you owe, follow-ups from your calls) beside what SleePO recommends you start (emails it has written, promises you made,
  next steps). It replaces the dashboard.
- **Follow-ups from your calls work again.** An update on 16 Nov stopped them being written after each meeting.
- **Granola and FluidVoice are recommended at install.** The installer and the doctor check say when either is missing,
  and what you lose without it.

## 23 Nov 2026
- **Ask SleePO can sort out files for you.** It makes folders, copies, moves and renames files, downloads a file from
  SharePoint or OneDrive and opens it. It shows each step first and does only the ones you allow.
- **Continue in Claude Desktop** opens the conversation's own folder, so Claude sees the conversation and its sources.

## 20 Nov 2026
- **Your company's code, when a question needs it.** If your company's git repositories are on your Mac, SleePO can search
  them to answer how the product behaves (roles, settings, limits), after the emery yates and product docs.
  See **Knowledge › Product › Code base**.

## 19 Nov 2026
- **Pictures in emails are read.** Screenshots pasted into an email and pictures attached to it go to the reply writer, so a
  reply can answer what they show.
- **Fill in the blanks in the chat.** A draft in Ask SleePO with blanks asks you a few quick questions, as the Inbox does.
- **The app stays quick while Ask SleePO answers**, however many SleePO tabs you have open.

## 18 Nov 2026
- **SleePO's code on GitHub**, with a README and screenshots for people who don't code.
