_How your emails and messages sound: purpose first or context first, short or full, formal or warm._

## Rules that apply everywhere
- Purpose or ask first; one ask per message, with an owner and a date.
- Short sentences, plain words.

## Calendar and meetings
- Meetings on the days and hours in your profile (System › Personalisation), your usual length by default.
- _Anything else: mornings kept free, meetings clustered, buffers._
